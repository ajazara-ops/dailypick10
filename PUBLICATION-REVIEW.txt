Rollback / limitations
- Before the first guarded write, code+Source rollback may restore reviewed branch
  publishing. After scan_state exists, blindly returning to main/root can publish
  coordinator/source files. Keep Actions/last good site and pause starts while
  reviewing recovery. Never restore old data or erase delivery/notification claims.
- Gates OFF stop future starts, not active jobs. Legacy fallback can duplicate
  notices. Unknown delivery can intentionally omit a notice; no exactly-once claim.
- Git/Pages are not atomic. Concurrent manual writes can race after final claim;
  public proof detects divergence and suppresses notices but cannot undo a submitted
  deployment. Active-run review and the existing concurrency slot remain required.
- Artifact retention/storage and API limits are not a universal zero-cost guarantee.
